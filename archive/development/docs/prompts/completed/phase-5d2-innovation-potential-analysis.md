## Innovation Assessment Criteria

### Technology Readiness
- **Emerging**: Experimental technologies with high potential
- **Developing**: Technologies in active development with clear benefits
- **Mature**: Established technologies with proven innovation potential
- **Declining**: Legacy technologies with limited innovation opportunity

### Innovation Impact
- **Breakthrough**: Fundamental changes to approach or capability
- **Significant**: Major improvements to existing processes or outcomes
- **Incremental**: Modest improvements with clear benefits
- **Minimal**: Small optimizations with limited impact

### Implementation Feasibility
- **High**: Can be implemented with current resources and timeline
- **Medium**: Requires additional resources or extended timeline
- **Low**: Significant investment or research required
- **Research**: Requires fundamental research before implementation
